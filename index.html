Free tools for making sense of what happens between two people

Small, open tools for the hard moments in a relationship. Each one runs in your browser, needs no account and sends nothing anywhere. English and Russian.

Tools

Relationship reflection journal

Journal prompts for the hard moments in a relationship: after a fight, before a hard conversation, jealousy, feeling distant, after a breakup, weekly check-in. One question at a time, private and offline.

Open →Source

Thoughts after a fight

A CBT thought record adapted to arguments with a partner: what happened, what you told yourself, feelings 0-100, thinking traps, facts for and against, a balanced thought. Runs in your browser, sends nothing.

Open →Source

Relationship questionnaires

Seven short questionnaires about what happens between two people: conflict patterns, effort balance, mental load, relationship clarity, contact changes, after a breakup. Open data and scoring, nothing is sent anywhere.

Open →Source

Psychology lenses

Describe a relationship situation and see how psychoanalysis, Jung, CBT, attachment theory, family systems, transactional analysis, Gottman and NVC would look at it. Explains the school, never judges the person.

Open →Source

16 associations

Write 16 words about one topic and merge them in pairs down to one word. A quiet self-reflection exercise that runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Open →Source

Not a therapist

A free system prompt and Claude skill for AI chats about relationships and feelings: listen first, no diagnoses, no labels for absent partners, no leave-or-stay verdicts, crisis signs first. With 14 test scenarios.

Open →Source

Couples counselling kit

Templates, homework sheets and AI prompts for psychologists and coaches who work with couples.

Дневник отношений

Вопросы для дневника на трудные моменты в отношениях: после ссоры, перед трудным разговором, ревность, отдаление, после расставания, еженедельная сверка. По одному вопросу, приватно и офлайн.

Мысли после ссоры

Дневник мыслей из КПТ для ссор с партнёром: что случилось, что я себе сказал, чувства 0-100, ловушки мышления, факты за и против, взвешенная мысль. Работает в браузере, ничего не отправляет.

Опросники об отношениях

Семь коротких опросников о том, что происходит между двумя людьми: конфликты, баланс вклада, ментальная нагрузка, ясность статуса, изменения в общении, после расставания. Открытые данные и подсчёт, ничего не отправляется.
